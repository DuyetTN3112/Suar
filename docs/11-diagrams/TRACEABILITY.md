## Evidence Status Vocabulary

- `Implemented`: active route/runtime and persisted behavior are verified. Optional configuration does not make an implemented runtime partial when fallback is intentional.
- `Partial`: some runtime exists, but delivery surface or claimed capability is incomplete.
- `Planned`: capability comes from roadmap/capability-model material and lacks enough current runtime evidence.
- `Removed`: retired product surface. It must not survive as a diagram family, appendix, or legacy flow.

Roadmap documents may explain direction, but cannot upgrade `Partial` or `Planned` behavior to `Implemented`.

## Removed-Surface Guard

- No diagram path may reintroduce generic conversation, chat room, participant, or message-delivery lifecycle.
- `task_review_messages` and `sprint_reverse_review_messages` are current typed review-workflow records. Their names do not authorize a generic messaging capability.
- Removed artifacts are deleted, not moved into `Legacy/`.
- Compatibility enum/field names may be documented only when current runtime still reads or writes them, and must be marked as compatibility data rather than product capability.

## Completion Definition

Rebuild completes only when:

1. every `.mmd`, `.bpmn`, `.puml`, or `.dmn` passes its family-specific rules in [`STANDARDS.md`](STANDARDS.md);
2. every source has a same-basename PNG, with no orphan PNG;
3. every PNG fits one A4 portrait or landscape page at `100%`, stays within target `width:height` ratio `0.75:1` to `4:1`, and remains readable without zoom;
4. every capability above has an honest `Implemented`, `Partial`, `Planned`, or `Removed` status backed by current evidence;
5. no diagram claims a nonexistent path, symbol, table, route, delivery surface, or state transition;
6. galleries list every source exactly once and all relative links resolve;
7. generic conversation/chat and other removed product surfaces do not remain as diagram families or legacy appendices;
8. Elasticsearch, project staffing/sprint delivery, rubric, task submission/evidence, and health/observability coverage remain explicit after future edits.
